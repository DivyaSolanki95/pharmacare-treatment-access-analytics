# Business Insights

## Executive Summary

The PharmaCare analysis evaluates patient treatment access from diagnosis through treatment initiation and discontinuation.

The analysis identified meaningful differences in treatment initiation and treatment timelines across insurance types and therapy areas.

## Key Findings

### 1. Treatment Initiation

Out of 10,000 diagnosed patients:

- 8,647 patients initiated treatment.
- Treatment initiation rate: 86.47%.
- 1,353 patients had not started treatment.

This indicates that the majority of diagnosed patients progressed to treatment, while a meaningful patient segment remained outside treatment initiation.

### 2. Treatment Timeline

The overall average time from diagnosis to treatment initiation was:

**28.87 days**

The analysis shows variation across therapy areas, with Neurology, Oncology, and Immunology showing longer average treatment timelines than Cardiology and Diabetes.

### 3. Insurance-Level Access Differences

Treatment initiation varied substantially by insurance type:

| Insurance Type | Treatment Initiation Rate |
|---|---:|
| Private Insurance | 90.93% |
| Employer Insurance | 89.44% |
| Government Insurance | 81.68% |
| Self Pay | 80.00% |

Government Insurance and Self Pay showed lower treatment initiation rates compared with Private and Employer Insurance.

### 4. Access Approval Delay

Average access approval time was approximately:

**17.09 days**

Variation was observed across regions and insurance groups, indicating potential differences in the patient access process.

### 5. Treatment Discontinuation

1,142 patients discontinued treatment.

This represents approximately **11.42% of the diagnosed patient population**.

Discontinuation rates also varied across therapy areas and insurance groups, highlighting segments that may require further investigation.

## Business Recommendations

1. Investigate barriers affecting treatment initiation among Government Insurance and Self Pay patients.
2. Investigate longer treatment timelines in Neurology, Oncology, and Immunology.
3. Review regional and payer-level access approval processes to identify operational bottlenecks.
4. Monitor discontinuation patterns by therapy and payer to identify segments requiring deeper analysis.
5. Use provider-level analysis to identify performance variation and opportunities for process improvement.

## Important Note

The dataset is synthetic and is intended for analytics demonstration purposes only. Findings should not be interpreted as real-world clinical or patient outcomes.
